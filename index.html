The projects are fictional. The ESM is a real institution and the institutional background here is accurate, but Project CASTOR, Project POLLUX, their people, budgets and figures are invented for teaching. Nothing here describes a real ESM project or a real ESM employee. The historical figures are memory aids, not portrayals. This is a study project and confers no certification.
