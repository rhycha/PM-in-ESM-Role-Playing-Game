# Start a PM² voice study conversation

Attach **[PM2_VOICE_STUDY_PACK.md](PM2_VOICE_STUDY_PACK.md)** to your conversation. It contains both your background reading and the tutor's instructions, scenarios and answer guidance. You only need this one attachment for the study sessions.

Send this message with it:

> Use the attached PM² voice study pack. I am a beginner. Teach me through a Socratic conversation in English, with short French bridges when useful. Start with Session 1. Explain the situation before asking me to decide. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer. If I am stuck, explain; if I ask for the direct answer, give it. Say numbers slowly with their units. At the end, give me the progress handoff from this pack.

Change the language in that message if you prefer. Italian is optional; learning PM² does not require practising Italian at the same time.

If your app offers voice, start it in the same conversation after the attachment is available. In ChatGPT Work or Codex, OpenAI describes **Start voice chat** for an existing task and **Start new voice chat** for a new one. Availability depends on rollout and account access. If voice is unavailable, the same pack works in text. [OpenAI voice instructions](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/features/voice)

## Understand the story first

**CASTOR builds a lending operations platform.** Staff currently retype payment information, checks take too long, and audit findings remain open. You play the Project Manager who must coordinate delivery, manage changes and obtain decisions from the right people.

**POLLUX builds a reporting portal using CASTOR's data.** Users disagree about their reporting needs. The team builds small usable pieces, receives feedback and adjusts priorities. It keeps PM² project governance while using agile development practices.

Both projects and their characters are fictional. The historical names are memory aids. You do not need to remember those names before you understand the roles.

## Use short sessions

Begin with Session 1 and spend about fifteen minutes. Read only the learner orientation beforehand if you want preparation; leave the tutor answer guidance until after your attempt.

Use any of these phrases during the conversation:

- “Explain the situation before the terminology.”
- “Say that number again with its unit.”
- “Give me a hint.”
- “Give me the direct answer, then another example.”
- “Use a French comparison for that term.”
- “Stop and give me the progress handoff.”

Save the handoff at the end. Paste it beside the attachment next time and ask to continue. It records what you actually explained and which point needs another attempt; it does not assume the next conversation remembers this one.
